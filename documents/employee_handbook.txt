Northstar Analytics Employee Handbook

Northstar Analytics supports hybrid work. Employees are expected to work from the Toronto office on Tuesday and Thursday. On other weekdays, employees may work remotely within Canada after agreeing on a schedule with their manager.

Full-time employees receive 20 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation requests longer than five consecutive business days should be submitted at least four weeks in advance. Employees submit requests in the HR portal, and their manager approves them.

The standard expense reimbursement limit for a client dinner is 75 CAD per attendee, excluding taxes and gratuity. Employees must submit an itemized receipt in the expense system within 30 days of the expense.

Information security incidents, including a suspected phishing email or a lost company laptop, must be reported immediately to security@northstar.example. Employees should not forward a suspicious email to colleagues.

The company provides a 1,200 CAD annual professional-development budget for eligible full-time employees. Eligible uses include courses, conferences, certification exams, and professional books. Manager approval is required before purchase.
